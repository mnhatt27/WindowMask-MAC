WINDOWMASK BY MYSTIC - MAC 1.1.3

Yeu cau: macOS 13 tro len, Photoshop 24.4 tro len, Creative Cloud Desktop.
Ho tro kien truc Apple Silicon (dong M) va Intel x86_64. Khong can Rosetta.

1. Luu tai lieu va dong Photoshop truoc khi cai/cap nhat.
2. Mo WindowMask Backend.pkg de cai backend vao /Applications.
3. Mo com.mystic.windowmask.mac_PS.ccx va xac nhan cai trong Creative Cloud.
   Panel trong DMG nay la ban 1.1.3, da sua quyen ket noi backend noi bo.
4. Mo Photoshop > Plugins > WINDOWMASK BY MYSTIC.
   Kiem tra dong Mac · v1.1.3 trong panel de chac da nap ban moi.
5. Nhap License Key, kich hoat va chon mot layer anh chua co mask.
6. Nhan TAO SOFT MASK. Lan dau co the can them thoi gian chon CPU/CoreML.

Backend tu khoi dong khi mo panel; khong can cai Python hay thu vien.
Anh duoc xu ly tai may, khong gui len server. License dung he thong kich hoat hien tai.
Khoa model va license duoc luu trong login Keychain; khong sao chep license Windows.
Neu macOS hoi quyen Keychain, chi chap nhan doi voi WindowMask da cai tu nguon tin cay.

BAN THU NGHIEM:
Neu ten DMG co UNNOTARIZED-TEST, ban do chua duoc ky/notarize de phan phoi.
Khong phat hanh cho khach truoc khi kiem thu Photoshop tren Mac va hoan tat ky Apple.
Khong tat Gatekeeper de cai ban thu nghiem.

Log: ~/Library/Application Support/WindowMaskMystic/Logs/backend.log
Cap nhat backend dung tien trinh ban cu bang helper native, khong can Python cu.
Cap nhat khong tu xoa tai lieu hay Keychain. Co the Undo mask bang Cmd+Z.
